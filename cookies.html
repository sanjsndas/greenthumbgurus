What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to enhance user experience, and to provide information to the website operators. Think of cookies as "digital breadcrumbs" that help a website remember you and your preferences.

Cookies serve a variety of functions. Some are essential for the basic operation of a website, while others help improve performance, remember your login details, or analyze how visitors interact with the site. Without cookies, many of the features that make the web convenient and personalized would not function properly.

Cookies are not harmful. They do not contain viruses, malware, or executable code. They cannot access your personal files or steal your identity. However, they do store information that can be used to identify your device and, in some cases, your browsing behavior. This is why we are committed to being transparent about our use of cookies and giving you control over them.

Types of Cookies

Cookies can be categorized in several ways:

  • Session Cookies: These are temporary cookies that are stored on your device only during your browsing session. They are deleted when you close your browser. Session cookies are essential for many website functions, such as keeping you logged in as you navigate between pages.
  • Persistent Cookies: These cookies remain on your device after you close your browser. They have an expiration date and are used to remember your preferences and actions across multiple visits to our website. For example, we may use persistent cookies to remember your language preference or to recognize you when you return.
  • First-Party Cookies: These are cookies set by the website you are visiting—in this case, GreenThumbGurus. They are used to remember your preferences and to improve your experience on our site.
  • Third-Party Cookies: These are cookies set by domains other than the one you are visiting. They are typically used for analytics, advertising, and social media integration. For example, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site, and these cookies are set by Google.

Do Cookies Compromise My Privacy?

Cookies themselves do not contain personally identifiable information, such as your name, address, or credit card details. However, they can be used in combination with other data to build a profile of your online behavior. At GreenThumbGurus, we do not use cookies to collect sensitive personal information. We only use them to enhance your experience and to understand how our website is used, always respecting your right to privacy.

Managing and Controlling Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or decline cookies. We provide several ways for you to manage your cookie preferences:

1. Cookie Consent Dialogue Box

When you first visit our website, you will see a cookie consent dialogue box at the bottom of the page. This box gives you the option to:

  • Accept All: This allows us to set all cookies, including analytics and functionality cookies, for an optimal browsing experience.
  • Decline All: This only sets strictly necessary cookies that are essential for the website to function. Performance, analytics, and functionality cookies will not be set.
  • Customize Settings: This allows you to choose which categories of cookies you wish to enable.

You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Preferences" link in the footer of our website or by using the consent box that appears when you revisit the site.

2. Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can choose to block all cookies, accept only first-party cookies, or delete existing cookies. Each browser has different settings, so we recommend consulting your browser's help documentation. Below are links to the cookie management pages for the most popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

3. Opt-Out Tools

For analytics cookies, you can also use opt-out tools provided by the service providers:

  • Google Analytics Opt-Out: You can install the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on to prevent Google Analytics from collecting and using your data.

Important Note on Blocking Cookies

If you choose to block or delete cookies, some features of our website may not function as intended. However, you will still be able to access the core content and information on our site.
